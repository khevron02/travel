Salamanca Saturday Market

Mooses on the Tasman Fountain

We took the bus to the top of Mt Wellington, but the clouds just never moved off of it, even by 2pm.

We're at the highest point on Mt Wellington:

Snowgum trees in the fog.

Visited Bruny Island with Fran!
at the Inala Gardens, saw 2 (two!) Tiger Snakes!

Saw an Echidna in the wild!

Visited the Port Arthur Prison (World Heritage Site).

Went the SCA Event Summer Picnic!

VIsit MONA - the Museum of Old and New Art. A real Funhouse!

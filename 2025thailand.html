to Thailand!


Traveled to Thailand on 10/24.

We flew VietJet Airlines from Da Nang, Vietnam.

Landing in Bangkok video link

We settled in our teensie hotel room in Khaosan, the tourist ghetto" but one of the top 10 places to visit in Bangkok, according to TripAdvisor. It's lots of Hostels, Hotels, Restaurants, Bars, Weekshops, Street vendors and all around noisy.


Walked around Saturday a bit, but took a motorized TukTuk Taxi back.

Took TukTuks to the Icon Siam Super Mall there and back.

Tuk-Tuk Ride Time Lapse video link

7 floors of stores, mostly higher-end than we'd ever buy, but it was fun. The food court was basically a whole floor of the mall, and had a mock-street-food area, serving inexpensive hand cooked fare. Had dinner there. Had Lunch at Bianca, an italian place on the 6th floor.

Bangkok skyline sample from the 7th floor:

We took the opportunity to watch a movie - TRON: Ares. Beautiful theater!
We hadn't been keeping up with movie releases. Liked this 3rd installment in the TRON franchise. Seems to set-up future possible pictures. But it was the only title we recognized.


Us in our Tuk-Tuk - 3 wheeled Crazy Taxi.

After some personal stuff, visited another mall. One floor had a San Francisco Theme! Other floors were London, Istanbul, Tokyo..

Visited Phra Sumen Fort / Park

Then decided to take a cheap and easy tour on the River

This is the big mall from the river

Had a birthday dinner at Mulligans Irish Bar.

The Democracy Monument

Made our way to Phuket, Thailand on 10-30 for some beach-time & snorkeling!
Had planned to arrive mid-afternoon and get to our Hotel in Karon in the daylight, but the flight was delayed 45 minutes, then, due to stormy weather, had to delay landing for 15 minutes, then it was rush hour traffic on the bus, and it took 3 hours to get to our place. Whew! But it was only 100 baht ($3.00ea)


Found food after 9pm at "EAT", yummy burger and tacos!
No food pic as we devoured them before remembering to take a picture!

Spent some time on the beach. The sun came out for us!


Spend Saturday afternoon (after rains) walking the town.
After lunch at an Italian place "Caio", Lareena got a foot cramp, so we turned into the very next Massage place
They are every 4th business on every block!
and got an hour each Thai Massage for $9.00 each!

Farewell Phuket

to Singapore!

Traveled to Singapore on 11/04/25
The famous indoor waterfall in the "Jewel", a huge glass bubble mall at the Airport.

We flew Singapore Airlines from Phuket, Thailand.

The Mooses liked the foliage and waterfall

Our hotel in Singapore

Decided to find some nature out of the city.
Took the Subway to the Merrymount station to hike in the Central Catchment Nature Reserve (MacRitchie) Jungle area.

Encountered MANY Macaques. Here's a mom with the clinging baby

A youngster.

Paved Jungle path. Most of the paths were off the ground, some were gravel, and LOTS of stairs

Link to:Youtube Video Walking the TreeTop Walk Bridge!

Link to:Youtube Video Time Lapse Descent from the Bridge.

Most of the Macaques were chill, but near the Jelutong Tower, one was a bit aggressive and followed us up to the top of the tower, hoping for a handout. They're obsessed if they see a plastic bag that may have food in it.

Looks friendly, but I didn't get a pic of him baring his teeth guarding against our escape down the spiral stair.

Toward the end of the hike, by the golf course.

Subway'd back into the city, found food, and then back to our room and crashed! Whew!

Friday we slept in late, went out and found lunch.. eventually. Did some shopping (Malls Everywhere!), and took some time Lapse and Video.
If you know me, you know I love my Tim-Tams! Here's a new version (to me). Yum!

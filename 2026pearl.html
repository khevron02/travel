To Pearl Harbor!

Entrance to Pearl Harbor - all features.

I... was a little salty about Pearl. First, it's not one 'park' or exhibit. It's 5.


It's Park Service, Private exhibit and Military - Navy base all together.
You could book in person, on-line, or take a tour company.
There's one entrance, and you can't bring anything larger than a small purse/tiny back in past the guard.
So, you had to PAY to check your larger bags here at the Bag Check.
Are there food vendors? Yes, but we didn't know.
These are not the first bits of information on the varioius and sundry web-sites or brochures.


Here's the information desk inside the 'park'. Lunchtime. Yep, information and the Arizona Memorial break for an hour lunch!

Visited Pearl Harbor. There are 5 attractions at Pearl.

Pearl Harbor Museum (didn't visit)

Bowfin Submarine tour


The museum part was very informative about equipment, tragedy, triumphs and history.

But it's the actual submarine tour that was the attraction. Close quarters indeed!








Arizona Battleship Memorial

THe Navy runs the shuttles to the Memorial. You can go stand-by, or get a number.
